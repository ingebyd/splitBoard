# App Review notes

Paste this into "Notes" in the App Review Information section. Reviewers often

steps come first.

---

SplitBoard is a keyboard extension for iPad. No account, no sign-in, no
in-app purchases, no server side.

How to enable it:

1. Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…
2. Under "Third-Party Keyboards" choose SplitBoard.
3. Open any text field, touch and hold the globe key and pick SplitBoard.

What to try:

- The key with the two-panel icon in the bottom row splits the keyboard into two
  halves pinned to the edges of the screen; tapping it again merges them back.
- The abc / абв key to the left of A switches between the English and Russian
  layouts. A short tap on the globe does the same; a long press leaves for
  another keyboard.
- Holding a letter shows alternate characters; swiping down on a key types the
  number or symbol printed in its corner.

Privacy:

- The keyboard does NOT request Full Access, so it cannot access the network.
- Nothing typed is stored or transmitted. The only saved settings are the split
  state and the current language, kept in the extension's own storage.

The app is iPad only: the split keyboard has no purpose on iPhone.
