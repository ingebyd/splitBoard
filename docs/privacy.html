SplitBoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

SplitBoard is a keyboard for iPad. It collects nothing about you.

No network access

SplitBoard does not ask for Full Access. iOS keeps network access away from keyboards that do not have it, so anything typed on SplitBoard stays on the iPad. The app has no networking code to begin with.

No analytics, no accounts

There is no analytics library, no advertising, no crash reporter and no sign-in of any kind.

What is stored on the iPad

The keyboard remembers two things in its own storage: whether you left it split or merged, and which of the two languages you were typing in. Deleting the app deletes both.

What you type

Text goes straight to the app you are typing in, the same way it does with the built-in keyboard. SplitBoard does not log it and does not keep a copy.

Children

Nothing is collected from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page and the date above is updated.

Contact

Questions and bug reports go to the project's issue tracker: github.com/ingebyd/splitBoard/issues
